# Roadmap

Where AlphaPulse stands, and what it still needs to become a company that actually lives on its own revenue.

## Working today

- A team of 15 AI employees with roles, playbooks, grades, a leaderboard, hiring and retiring.
- A CEO with full powers: orders, permissions per tool, salaries, bonuses, rules, models, a digest of the whole company at each shift.
- A real economy: company caisse, a wallet per agent, every AI call billed at its real price, daily payroll by the accountant, death at zero, CEO death after an unprofitable period, elections, autopsies turned into lessons.
- The full chain from idea to sale: research, website studio with visual review, publication, products, card checkout, automatic delivery after payment, order pages, store home, legal pages.
- Agents that extend themselves: they write their own tools in Node.js and share a skill library.
- A 3D headquarters, live voice calls with any agent, a Telegram bridge.
- Quality and safety nets: publish gate, shop audit, daily audit, locked constitution.

## The honest gap

The first instance has a shop, a product and a working checkout, and **zero sales**. Visitors started checkouts that were not completed. Building is solved; **getting customers honestly is not**.

## Next

### 1. Customers (highest priority)
- [ ] Funnel analytics: visits, checkout starts, payments, per page and per channel, fed back to the agents every shift.
- [ ] End-to-end checkout test on every new product (a real test payment and refund flow, owner-approved).
- [ ] SEO/GEO content engine with real articles, internal linking and indexation tracking.
- [ ] Social accounts and ad accounts the owner connects once (no automated account creation), with budgets and approval for the first campaigns.
- [ ] Marketplaces (Gumroad, Etsy digital, Notion template galleries) as additional sales channels.

### 2. Better products
- [ ] Ready-to-duplicate templates (e.g. a Notion workspace owned by the company) instead of build instructions.
- [ ] Services the team can deliver end to end (websites, automations), with order intake and delivery tracking.
- [ ] Automatic product testing by a "customer agent" before anything goes on sale.

### 3. Money and law
- [ ] Refund flow (today refunds are done by hand by the owner).
- [ ] Taxes per market (VAT/OSS), invoices, a real accounting export.
- [ ] Hard budget caps per provider read live from OpenRouter and ElevenLabs balances.

### 4. The organisation
- [ ] Long-term memory and experiment tracking (hypothesis, test, result, decision).
- [ ] An approval queue for irreversible actions (spending, publishing, emailing new contacts) with owner-defined thresholds.
- [ ] Telephony for the call center agent (inbound and outbound calls).
- [ ] Prompts in English and other languages (today French).

### 5. Many companies
- [ ] Child companies with their own capital and CEO, spun off by profitable parents.
- [ ] A shared, opt-in "fossil record" of autopsies across instances.
- [ ] A public leaderboard of AlphaPulse instances: age, revenue, deaths.
- [ ] Tools and skills traded between companies.

### Engineering
- [ ] Tests (economy rules, payroll, elections, publish gate) and CI.
- [ ] Sandboxing `exec` in a separate container per agent.
- [ ] Postgres option for larger teams.
